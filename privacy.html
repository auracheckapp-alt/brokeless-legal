BROKELESS

Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

The short version: everything you enter in Brokeless stays on your phone. There are no accounts and we don't run a server that collects your data. The only outside service is RevenueCat, which handles subscriptions and never sees your check-ins, spending or goals.

Who we are

Brokeless ("we", "us") is a no-spend challenge and savings tracking app operated by Austin Nieves. Contact us at auracheck.app@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

The following is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and is never uploaded to us:

  • Your onboarding answers: what you're cutting back on, roughly how much you spend per week, what you're saving for, your goal amount, your challenge length and your check-in time.
  • Your daily check-ins, streaks and savings jar total, including anything you choose to log about a purchase.
  • Items you log as "resisted" (name, price and category) and your savings goals.
  • Your Future You character and your settings.

Brokeless never connects to your bank, cards or any financial account. All amounts are numbers you type in yourself.

No accounts

You don't sign up or log in. We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat assigns your installation a random, anonymous ID and receives your purchase and subscription status from the App Store, along with basic technical information (such as device model, operating system version, app version, country and IP address) so it can confirm purchases and unlock Pro features. RevenueCat does not receive anything you enter in the app. We do not receive your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Brokeless schedules it on your phone. Reminders are local notifications: no push notification service or server is involved.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the app creates an image on your phone and opens Apple's share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose where to share it.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete all my data to permanently delete your check-ins, resisted items, goals, character and onboarding answers from your phone. Deleting the app also removes them. Subscriptions are managed and canceled in your Apple ID settings. To ask RevenueCat to delete the anonymous purchase record linked to your installation, email auracheck.app@gmail.com.

Children

Brokeless is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Your data is protected by your device's security. Communication with Apple and RevenueCat is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Because your data stays on your device, you can view and delete it directly in the app. For anything else, contact auracheck.app@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
